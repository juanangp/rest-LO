# rest-LO

Personal simulations of LiquidO with REST: this is not the official repository of the LiquidO collaboration.

Simulations of LiquidO (opaque scintillator crossed by wavelength-shifting fibres read by SiPMs) with
[rest-for-physics-toolkit](https://github.com/rest-for-physics-toolkit/rest-framework), the new version of
REST-for-Physics. It replaces the legacy simulations of
[juanangp/losimulations](https://github.com/juanangp/losimulations) (`liquidO.rml`, `liquidO.sh`,
`macros/Geant4ToSignal.C`): YAML configurations with `parameters` instead of environment variables, the
fibres in the Geant4 geometry, and the macro that converted the simulated events into tracks replaced by a
chain of processes (`TRestFibreReadout` and the light processes of the detector library: scintillation and
light collection). There are no experimental data yet: only simulations.

| File | Content |
|---|---|
| `geometry/` | The chamber (a cube of `chamberSize`, from z = 0, filled with the scintillator `material`) in its copper cover; the fibres (`fibreVolume`, polystyrene of 1 mm diameter) are placed inside the chamber by the entity `fibres`, the placements of the readout of rest-common-readouts. |
| `simulations/liquidO.yaml` | restG4 simulation; parameter `generator` (Gammas, SolarAxions5MeV, SNAxions, CRY, chosen with a `select` block as the `if` of `liquidO.rml`), `material`, `chamberSize`, `coverSize`, `nEvents` and those of the generators (`energy`, `model`, `exposure`). |
| `simulations/physicsLists.yaml` | The physics lists, included by the restG4 configurations. |
| `simulations/distributions/SNGammas.root` | Energy of the gammas of the supernova axions (generator SNAxions). |
| `analysis/Geant4ToTracks.yaml` | restManager chain: quenching, scintillation, capture of the light by the fibres (Gaussian light ball of 10 mm), SiPMs, electronics, hits and tracks. |

The materials and the fibre readout (`LiquidO.root`, readout `LiquidO_Readout`: fibres along Y and along X
of pitch 20 mm in alternated layers 10 mm apart, in a box of 1500 mm, dummy identity decoding) and the
placements of its fibres in the geometry (`LiquidO_Readout_fibres.xml`) are those of
[rest-common-materials](https://github.com/rest-for-physics-toolkit/rest-common-materials) and
[rest-common-readouts](https://github.com/rest-for-physics-toolkit/rest-common-readouts) v1.0, read by URL
(downloaded once and cached in `$REST_HOME/.rest/download`). Another chamber size needs another readout
(`chamberSize` and the readout must have the same size).

## Chain

The commands below are launched from the directory of the outputs, e.g. the parent of this repository (the
geometry and the distributions are relative to the YAML, the outputs to the launch directory). The `.root`
and `.log` files are not committed (`.gitignore`), except the distributions.

```bash
# 1. A simulation
restG4 rest-LO/simulations/liquidO.yaml --set generator=Gammas --set energy=10 --set nEvents=100 \
    2>&1 | tee sim.log

# 2. From the simulated events to tracks
restManager --c rest-LO/analysis/Geant4ToTracks.yaml --i Run<number>_restG4_Gammas_10MeV_1500mm_<user>.root \
    2>&1 | tee tracks.log
```

To change the parameters of the light, edit `analysis/Geant4ToTracks.yaml`: `lightYield` and the
`scintillation` time profile (per particle) of the liquid, the `wls` response of the fibres (`Y11`:
attenuation and speed along them, re-emission time), `captureProbability` of `lightCollection` (the
capture by a fibre, a formula of the distance to it in mm, or `"file.root:graph"` from an optical
simulation of Geant4) and its `quantumEfficiency`, and the `timeJitter` and `gainSpread` of the SiPMs in
`lightToSignal`. The light reaches the read end of each fibre (`readoutEnd` of the readout). The fibres must
not intersect: the two families are 10 mm apart in Z.

### Cosmic rays with CRY

The generator `CRY` simulates the showers of CRY, generated by restG4 during the simulation (restG4 built with
CRY): each event is a shower with all its particles, with their times. The showers start on a horizontal
square of 3 m (`subBoxLength`) centred above the chamber, at y = 800 mm, and go down along -Y. The options of
CRY are in the section `cry` of `liquidO.yaml` (class `TRestGeant4CRY`; the generator only places the
square): the particles returned, the site (`latitude`, `altitude`, `date`), `subBoxLength` and
`maxExposureTime`. The simulation stops at `nEvents` or when CRY has simulated the time `exposure`:

```bash
restG4 rest-LO/simulations/liquidO.yaml --set generator=CRY --set nEvents=100000 2>&1 | tee sim.log
restG4 rest-LO/simulations/liquidO.yaml --set generator=CRY --set nEvents=0 --set exposure="1 h" \
    2>&1 | tee sim.log                                                    # only the exposure stops it
```

The output stores the exposure simulated (`TRestGeant4Metadata::GetCRYExposureTime`, us), to normalize to a
rate.

Only some particles: `particles` of the section `cry` lists those returned by CRY, of `muons`, `neutrons`,
`protons`, `gammas`, `electrons` (electrons and positrons), `pions` and `kaons` (all by default). The showers
keep only those particles, and the exposure is the same (the time simulated by CRY does not depend on the
particles kept), so the rates of each kind can be compared. E.g.

```yaml
    particles: ["muons"]                  # only the muons
    particles: ["neutrons"]               # only the neutrons
    particles: ["gammas", "electrons"]    # the electromagnetic component
    particles: ["muons", "neutrons"]      # muons and neutrons
```

## Differences with the legacy simulations

- The cover and the chamber are centred at the same point (legacy: the chamber 0.05 mm higher).
- The cosmic rays are the showers of CRY (generator `CRY`), instead of single muons (`CosmicMuons`) or
  neutrons (`CosmicNeutrons`) from analytic distributions shot from the sphere of the whole world.
- The fibres are dead material in Geant4: the energy deposited in them gives no light.
- The quenching follows Birks law (kB = 0.1 mm/MeV) instead of a factor 0.1 for the ions.
